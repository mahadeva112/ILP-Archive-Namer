What this design does
  • 1Shows the date the way the name will. The field reads 12-Mar-2021, never the PC's locale format, plus the weekday and how long ago it was.
  • 2Old dates are fast. Click the month to choose from 12 months, or the year to jump through years. No clicking back 60 times to reach 2021.
  • 3Type in any common form. 12/3/21, 12 mar 2021, 2021-03-12, today, yesterday. Day comes first, as in India.
  • 4Future dates are blocked. An archive date can't be tomorrow, so those days are struck through.
  • 5Keyboard first. Arrows move, PgUp/PgDn change month, T jumps to today, Enter picks, Esc closes.
